Things to remember

Little notes for your place in the saga

  • Wear the coloursMaenziraat in pink & blue, Devgon in yellow & green, and the Lagan in your festive finest.
  • Mind the hoursDevgon begins at 8 AM. Maenziraat and the Lagan glow from 7 PM.
  • Two venues, one sagaKashmiri Bhawan, Sector 37 for Maenziraat and Devgon. Rajhans, Surajkund for the Lagan.
  • Stay for the petalsPosh Puja closes the Lagan. Every guest showers the couple with flowers, as if upon Shiva and Parvati.
  • Sing alongAt Maenziraat the wanvun flows late into the night. No lyrics needed, only joy.
  • Tell the storyEvery moment you capture becomes a page of ours. Share it with #ShivGauriSaga.
